Our ideia was to create a global monitor to help anyone, from all range of expertise, experience or novice developers or DBA, to be able to investigate and analyze global metrics through a chat like web app. Using AI as it’s engine. 

So here we have our global watcher, we can ask question about the Globals in our system, for example, “tell me the biggest global that we have in our system.” or “can you show me the global with the biggest growth rate in the last 15 days.”

Under the hood, we’re taking periodic snapshots of global sizes and locations. We vectorize this data so the AI can recognize patterns—like two different globals growing at the same unusual pace. The best part? It’s fully configurable. You can swap the AI agent or tweak the logic right in the application.properties.
With Global Watcher, we’re moving from static monitoring to proactive conversation. It’s about making system insights accessible to everyone on the team. Thanks for watching, and we hope you enjoy exploring Global Watcher!"
